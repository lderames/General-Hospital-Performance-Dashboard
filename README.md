# Introduction
📊 This suite of dashboards provides a comprehensive view of healthcare operations, focusing on patient demographics, mortality rate, medical outcomes, financial claims, and overall hospital performance. By integrating key metrics across multiple dimensions, these dashboards empower administrators, healthcare professionals, and financial teams to make data-driven decisions that improve patient care, optimize resources, and ensure financial sustainability.

# Background
🦾**DataSense PH** has launched an exciting **Data Challenge** for aspiring data professionals and enthusiasts, providing a platform to showcase their analytical and visualization skills. Participants are given structured datasets and tasked with developing dashboards that uncover key trends and actionable insights.

# Tools Used
- 📊 Power BI Desktop - Main data visualiztion tool for report creation.
- 📁 Power Query - Data transformation and cleaning layer for reshaping and preparing data.
- 🧠 DAX ( Data Analysis Expression) - Used for calculated measures, dynamic visuals, and conditional logic.
- 📋 Data Modeling - Relationship established among tables to enable filtering and aggregation.

# Features / Highlights
 - ## Business Problem

### **Hospital Patient Report Dashboard**  
- **Tracking Patient Survival Rates** – Monitor changes in survival rates over time to assess improvements in treatments.  
- **Understanding Demographic Impact on Health Outcomes** – Analyze how factors like age, gender, and race affect survival trends.  
- **Identifying High-Risk Patient Groups** – Determine which patient demographics have higher mortality rates and require targeted interventions.  
- **Optimizing Resource Allocation for Patient Care** – Assess patient distributions to improve hospital staffing and resource management.

### **Hospital Procedure Report Dashboard**  
- **Analyzing Procedure Cost Efficiency** – Identify which procedures have the highest costs and assess their profitability for hospitals.  
- **Understanding Turnaround Time Trends** – Track procedure turnaround times to optimize hospital efficiency.  
- **Identifying High-Demand Medical Procedures** – Pinpoint procedures with the most patient demand and adjust resources accordingly.  
- **Evaluating Impact of Encounter Class on Expenditures** – Compare costs between emergency vs. outpatient encounters to refine financial strategies.

### **Hospital Claims Report Dashboard**  
- **Tracking Claim Approval Rates** – Analyze trends in claim approvals versus denials to optimize insurance processing.  
- **Assessing Insurance Provider Impact on Claim Amounts** – Identify which insurance types contribute the most to hospital revenue.  
- **Detecting Seasonal Claim Trends** – Monitor fluctuations in claims based on external factors (e.g., flu season, pandemic impacts).  
- **Analyzing Common Reasons for Claims** – Determine the most frequent medical conditions linked to claims to anticipate demand for treatments.


 - ## Goal of the dashboard

### **Hospital Patient Report Dashboard**  
**Goal:** To analyze patient demographics and survival rates, helping healthcare providers identify trends in mortality and optimize patient care strategies.

### **Hospital Procedure Report Dashboard**  
**Goal:** To evaluate hospital procedures in terms of cost, turnaround time, and demand, enabling hospitals to improve operational efficiency and financial management.

### **Hospital Claims Report Dashboard**  
**Goal:** To track hospital claims data, insurance distributions, and common claim reasons, assisting in optimizing revenue cycle management and improving claim approval processes.
